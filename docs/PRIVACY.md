# PuzzlePop privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

PuzzlePop is a jigsaw puzzle app for Android that turns your own photos into
puzzles. It's made to be safe to hand to a child, and it collects nothing.

## The short version

- No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
- The app has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.
- Your photos and everything else stay on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

PuzzlePop keeps these on your device, in its own private app storage:

- **Photos you add.** When you pick a photo from your gallery or take one with
  the camera, the app saves its own copy so it can cut it into a puzzle.
- **Puzzles and progress.** Saved puzzles, how far you got, and best times.
- **Settings.** Kid-mode time limits, puzzle sizes, language and similar choices.
- **The grown-up PIN.** Stored only as a salted hash, never as the PIN itself.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It isn't included in Android's
cloud backup or device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app, or clearing
its data in Android's settings, deletes all of it.

## Permissions

- **Photos:** the app uses Android's photo picker, so it only ever sees the
  photos you choose. It can't browse your gallery.
- **Camera:** photos are taken with your device's own camera app. PuzzlePop
  doesn't have the camera permission.
- **Fingerprint / biometrics:** used, if you choose, to unlock grown-up
  settings. Android does the check; the app only learns whether it succeeded
  and never sees your fingerprint.

## Children

PuzzlePop is designed for families, including children under 13. It collects
no personal information from anyone, children included. It has no ads, no
chat and no in-app purchases, and no links out of the app in kid mode. The only link to a web
page (this policy) is in grown-up settings, behind the PIN.

## Changes
